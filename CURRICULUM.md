# Read the book

Chapters 1–3 build a compact Python bridge. Chapters 4–9 move through tensors, gradients, model training, evaluation and pretrained adaptation. Chapters 10–18 cover data, retrieval and operation. Chapters 19–25 are deeper studies, and Chapter 26 is the draft capstone. Later studies are under revision.
